# Optimization Workflows

Step-by-step workflows for optimizing content across channels, plus persuasion frameworks for conversion-focused writing.

## Blog Post Optimization

### Workflow
1. **Keyword Research** — Identify 1 primary + 2-3 secondary keywords. Check search volume, difficulty, and intent.
2. **Title Optimization** — Include primary keyword. Use a headline formula (how-to, number, question). Keep under 60 characters.
3. **Meta Description** — Under 160 characters. Include primary keyword. Compel the click with a clear benefit or curiosity gap.
4. **Content Structure** — H1 (title), H2s for main sections, H3s for subsections. One core idea per section.
5. **First Paragraph** — Include primary keyword naturally. Hook with stat, question, bold claim, or scenario. State what the post covers.
6. **Body Optimization** — Short paragraphs (2-4 sentences). Subheadings every 200-300 words. At least one data point, example, or quote per section.
7. **Internal Linking** — Link to 2-3 related posts on your site. Use descriptive anchor text (not "click here").
8. **External Linking** — 1-2 links to authoritative sources. Opens in new tab.
9. **Readability Pass** — Target 8th-grade reading level. Active voice. Front-load key information. Break up long paragraphs.
10. **CTA Integration** — One primary CTA at the end. Optional in-line CTAs where contextually relevant.
11. **Image Optimization** — Descriptive alt text with keyword where natural. Compress images. Add captions.

### Blog Post Checklist
- [ ] Primary keyword in title, first paragraph, one H2, meta description, URL slug
- [ ] Secondary keywords in body and subheadings
- [ ] Hook in first 1-3 sentences
- [ ] 3-5 sections with descriptive H2 headings
- [ ] Data, examples, or quotes in each section
- [ ] 2-3 internal links, 1-2 external links
- [ ] Meta description under 160 characters
- [ ] Reading level appropriate for audience
- [ ] One clear CTA

## Landing Page Optimization

### Workflow
1. **Value Proposition** — Clear headline under 10 words stating the primary benefit. Subheadline adds supporting context.
2. **Hero Section** — Headline + subheadline + primary CTA + supporting visual. Above the fold.
3. **Social Proof** — Add within first scroll: testimonials, customer logos, user count, rating, case study snippet.
4. **Value Propositions** — 3-4 benefit-driven sections. Lead with outcome, not feature. Use icons or visuals.
5. **Persuasion Framework** — Structure body using AIDA, PAS, or Before/After/Bridge (see Persuasion Frameworks below).
6. **Objection Handling** — FAQ section or trust signals addressing top 3-5 buyer concerns.
7. **Form Optimization** — Minimize fields (name + email minimum). Label clearly. Add privacy reassurance near submit.
8. **CTA Placement** — Primary CTA above fold and repeated at bottom. Use action verbs + value ("Start free trial — no credit card needed").
9. **Mobile Optimization** — Test responsive layout. Thumb-friendly buttons. Fast load (under 3 seconds).
10. **A/B Test Plan** — Identify 2-3 elements to test: headline, CTA copy, hero image, social proof placement.

### Landing Page Checklist
- [ ] Headline states primary benefit in under 10 words
- [ ] Primary CTA visible without scrolling
- [ ] Social proof within first scroll
- [ ] 3-4 benefit sections (not feature lists)
- [ ] FAQ or objection handling section
- [ ] CTA repeated at bottom
- [ ] Mobile-responsive and fast-loading
- [ ] Form fields minimized

## Social Media Optimization

### Workflow
1. **Platform Adaptation** — Adjust format, length, and tone for target platform (see content-templates.md for platform specs).
2. **Engagement Hook** — First line must stop the scroll. Use: surprising stat, bold claim, question, or contrarian take.
3. **Body Structure** — 2-4 concise points or a short narrative. Use line breaks for readability.
4. **Visual Planning** — Select or create a complementary image, carousel, or video. Native content outperforms links.
5. **Hashtag Strategy** — Research relevant hashtags. LinkedIn: 3-5. Twitter/X: 1-3. Instagram: 5-15.
6. **CTA** — Tell the reader what to do: comment, click, share, save, tag someone.
7. **Timing** — Post during peak audience activity. Test and track optimal times.
8. **Engagement Plan** — Reply to comments within first hour. Engage with related posts. Build community.

### Platform Quick Rules

| Platform | Length Sweet Spot | Key Tactic |
|----------|-----------------|------------|
| **LinkedIn** | 1,300 characters | Personal stories + lessons learned |
| **Twitter/X** | 280 characters (threads for long) | Strong opening + punchy points |
| **Instagram** | <125 chars before "more" | Visual-first + storytelling captions |
| **Facebook** | <80 chars for links | Questions drive comments |

## Email Optimization

### Workflow
1. **Subject Line** — Under 50 characters. Create curiosity or state clear value. Test 2-3 variations.
2. **Preview Text** — Complement the subject line (don't repeat it). Add context that drives opens.
3. **Header** — Visual anchor + one-line value statement.
4. **Body** — 2-3 scannable content blocks. Bold intro sentences. Short paragraphs. One primary message.
5. **CTA** — One clear action per email. Visually distinct button. Action verb + specific outcome.
6. **Personalization** — Use name, company, or behavior data where available. Segment by audience.
7. **Mobile Test** — Preview on mobile. Most email is read on mobile. Ensure buttons are thumb-friendly.
8. **Send Time** — Test optimal send times for your audience. Avoid Monday mornings and Friday afternoons.

## Persuasion Frameworks

### AIDA (Attention — Interest — Desire — Action)

| Stage | Goal | Tactics |
|-------|------|---------|
| **Attention** | Stop the scroll | Bold headline, surprising stat, striking visual |
| **Interest** | Build engagement | Relevant problem, relatable scenario, "did you know" |
| **Desire** | Create want | Benefits, social proof, transformation story |
| **Action** | Drive conversion | Clear CTA, urgency, risk reduction |

**Best for:** Landing pages, sales emails, product pages, ads.

### PAS (Problem — Agitate — Solve)

| Stage | Goal | Tactics |
|-------|------|---------|
| **Problem** | Identify the pain | Name the specific frustration the reader faces |
| **Agitate** | Amplify urgency | Show consequences of inaction, quantify the cost |
| **Solve** | Present the answer | Position your solution as the clear fix, show proof |

**Best for:** Blog intros, email copy, social posts, case study openings.

### Before / After / Bridge

| Stage | Goal | Tactics |
|-------|------|---------|
| **Before** | Paint current state | Describe the reader's situation now (the problem) |
| **After** | Paint desired state | Describe life after the solution (the outcome) |
| **Bridge** | Connect the two | Show how your product/service gets them there |

**Best for:** Case studies, testimonial pages, transformation-focused content.

## Readability Standards

| Metric | Target | Tool |
|--------|--------|------|
| **Flesch Reading Ease** | 60-80 | Hemingway, Readable |
| **Average sentence length** | 15-20 words | Manual count or tool |
| **Paragraph length** | 3-4 sentences max | Visual scan |
| **Subheading frequency** | Every 300-400 words | Word count between H2s |
| **Reading level** | 8th grade (broad audience) | Flesch-Kincaid |

### Readability Quick Fixes
- Split sentences over 25 words
- Replace jargon with plain language
- Convert passive to active voice
- Break paragraphs longer than 4 sentences
- Add subheadings to any section over 300 words
- Use bullet points for 3+ related items

## Conversion Elements

| Element | Where | Why |
|---------|-------|-----|
| **Above-fold CTA** | Landing pages, product pages | Don't make users scroll to act |
| **Trust indicators** | Near CTAs, checkout, forms | Reduce anxiety at decision point |
| **Social proof** | Throughout, especially mid-page | Build credibility progressively |
| **Urgency signals** | CTAs, headers (when genuine) | Motivate action now vs. later |
| **Risk reduction** | Near CTAs, pricing | Remove barriers ("free trial", "cancel anytime") |
| **Specificity** | Headlines, value props, proof | Specific claims are more believable than vague ones |

## Content Audit Quick Process

1. **Inventory** — List all content pieces with URL, type, date, target keyword
2. **Score** — Rate each on traffic, engagement, conversion, freshness (1-5)
3. **Categorize** — Update (high potential, needs refresh), Keep (performing well), Cut (low value, outdated), Consolidate (overlapping topics)
4. **Prioritize** — Update high-potential pieces first; they have the fastest ROI
5. **Schedule** — Set refresh cadence: quarterly for high-traffic, annually for evergreen
