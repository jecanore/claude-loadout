# Headlines, Hooks & CTAs

Proven formulas for attention-grabbing headlines, compelling hooks, and effective calls to action.

## Headline Formulas

### Core Formulas

| Formula | Example |
|---------|---------|
| How to [result] [without obstacle] | "How to Double Your Email Open Rates Without Sending More Emails" |
| [Number] [adj] ways to [result] | "7 Proven Ways to Reduce Customer Churn" |
| Why [common belief] is wrong | "Why More Content Is Not the Answer (And What to Do Instead)" |
| The [adj] guide to [topic] | "The Complete Guide to B2B Content Marketing" |
| [Do this], not [that] | "Build a Community, Not Just an Audience" |
| What [result] taught us about [topic] | "What 10,000 A/B Tests Taught Us About Email Subject Lines" |
| [Topic]: what [audience] needs to know | "SEO: What Marketers Need to Know in 2026" |
| [Option A] vs [Option B] | "Email Marketing vs Social Media: Which Drives More Leads?" |
| [Old way] is dead. Here's [new way] | "Cold Calling is Dead. Here's How to Generate Leads Now" |

### Headline Length

| Platform | Target |
|----------|--------|
| Google title tag | 50-60 characters |
| Blog post headline | 60-70 characters |
| Email subject line | 40-50 characters |
| Facebook | <40 characters for max engagement |
| LinkedIn | 150-200 characters |
| Instagram | <125 characters before "more" |

### Power Words

**Action:** Transform, Discover, Unlock, Boost, Eliminate, Master
**Curiosity:** Secret, Hidden, Revealed, Behind-the-Scenes, Insider
**Fear:** Warning, Mistake, Trap, Killer, Danger
**Urgency:** Now, Today, Immediate, Quick, Limited
**Authority:** Proven, Tested, Research-Backed, Expert-Approved, Data-Driven

### Headline Validation Checklist
- [ ] Primary keyword included naturally
- [ ] Benefit to reader is clear
- [ ] Specific (numbers, timeframes, concrete details)
- [ ] Appropriate length for platform
- [ ] Would you click this in a feed?

## Hook Formulas (Opening Lines)

Every piece of content lives or dies on the first 1-3 sentences.

| Type | Formula | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Surprising stat** | "[Unexpected number] of [audience] [fact]." | "73% of marketers say their biggest challenge is not budget — it is focus." |
| **Contrarian** | "[Common wisdom] is wrong. Here's why." | "The best marketing campaigns start with saying no to most channels." |
| **Question** | "When was the last time [relatable scenario]?" | "When was the last time a marketing email actually changed what you bought?" |
| **Scenario** | "Imagine [desirable outcome] before [it happens]." | "Imagine knowing which messages will land before your campaign goes live." |
| **Bold claim** | "Most [things] fail because of [surprising reason]." | "Most landing pages lose half their visitors in the first three seconds." |
| **Story opening** | "Last [time period], [specific situation]. Here's what we did." | "Last quarter, our team spent 20 hours a week on reporting. Here's what we changed." |

### Hook Anti-Patterns
- Starting with a dictionary definition
- "In today's fast-paced world..." (AI cliche)
- Burying the lead in the third paragraph
- Opening with background that doesn't hook

## CTA Best Practices

### Principles
1. **Action verbs:** "Get", "Start", "Download", "Join", "Try", "See"
2. **Be specific:** "Start your free trial" beats "Submit"
3. **Create urgency (when genuine):** "Join 500 teams already using this"
4. **Reduce risk:** "No credit card required", "Cancel anytime", "Free for 14 days"
5. **One primary CTA** per page or email — too many choices reduce conversions

### CTA by Context

| Context | Primary CTA Examples | Secondary CTA |
|---------|---------------------|---------------|
| **Blog post** | "Subscribe for weekly insights" | "Read our complete guide to [topic]" |
| **Landing page** | "Start free trial" / "Get a demo" | "See pricing" / "Watch video" |
| **Email** | "Read the full story" / "Claim your spot" | "Reply and tell us" |
| **Social media** | "Drop a comment" / "Save this for later" | "Link in bio" |
| **Case study** | "See how [product] works for your team" | "Talk to our team" |

### CTA Placement

| Location | Rule |
|----------|------|
| **Landing page** | Above the fold — don't make users scroll to act |
| **Email** | After establishing value — not in the first sentence |
| **Blog post** | At the end — after earning the reader's trust |
| **Long-form page** | Repeat primary CTA at bottom |
| **In-line** | When contextually relevant (e.g., mention related guide) |

### CTA Button Copy

**Strong:** Start free trial, Get the guide, Book a demo, See pricing, Create account
**Weak:** Submit, Click here, Learn more, Continue, Next

Add context when possible: "Start free trial — no credit card needed" is stronger than "Start free trial" alone.

## A/B Testing Headlines

### What to Test
- Short vs long headlines
- Question vs statement
- Specific numbers vs round numbers
- Emotional vs rational language
- "You" vs third person

### Methodology
1. Change only one element per test
2. Run until 95% statistical confidence
3. Minimum 1,000 views per variation
4. Test for at least 1 week
5. Apply winning patterns to future content
